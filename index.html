    A LITTLE PROMPT CRAFT

    Better instructions.
    More useful first drafts.

    Choose a technique to shape how your prompt guides an AI assistant. This app generates the prompt only; it never sends your data to an AI service.

    01

    Zero-shot

    Give a clear task and context, without examples. Useful when requirements are straightforward.

    “Recommend a monthly trend view for these fields.”
    02

    Few-shot

    Show a small example of the answer structure you want. Helpful for consistent, scannable output.

    “KPI → definition → required fields → caveat.”
    03

    Role-based

    Set a relevant expert perspective to guide the depth and framing of the response.

    “Act as an experienced Power BI developer…”
